# Metro Solidarity

*Syntagma Square (Πλατεία Συντάγματος), Athens, Greece*

Like other metropolitan cities around the world serviced by metro, Athens has a subreddit group that informs people of its daily underground activities.

Yet, more than that, the people have solidarity enough to leave tickets with minutes left for others in need to pick up outside the turnstiles of stations. People check the subreddit for reports of ticket checkers before deciding whether to purchase a ticket or risk getting caught with an expired one.
